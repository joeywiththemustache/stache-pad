Sustenance and Libations

"The only thing separating a Fire Island share house from a crack house is dinner."

You can get sustenance for you and your friends on Fire Island a number of ways, and I'm not going to recommend any of them over the others because the Pines have ears, and I prefer to cause other kinds of drama.

Pines Pantry (Very convenient!)

Located right in the harbor, the Pantry offers everything from a tasty breakfast sandwich and coffee (the cold brew is STRONG!) to fancy imported pastas to your favorite flavor of Swiss Navy. The cookies are well worth the hype and the merch makes a great gift.

Grocery Delivery: Instacart + Sayville Ferry Service (same day)
  1. Go to Instacart.com and make an account.
  2. Choose your store (I like Costco and Aldi, but you can get more special things from Stop & Shop and even the Dollar Store!)
  3. If you're not 100% sure, give Sayville Ferry Service a call (+1 631-589-0810) to check the boat situation for groceries that day and make sure your order gets there well before the cutoff.
  4. Place your order on Instacart for:

    [YOUR NAME]
    c/o Sayville Ferry Service
    41 River Road
    Sayville, NY 11782

    with these delivery instructions:
    "Please deliver to 41 River Road (Sayville Ferry Service) and label all bags/boxes with [YOUR NAME], PINES. Find a Sayville Ferry Service employee and tell them the groceries are for the next boat to Fire Island Pines."
  5. Show up to the dock with plenty of time AND CART(S) to get the groceries off the boat. If you don't have an account with Sayville Ferry Service, you'll need to pay in CASH!

WARNING: IF YOU DON'T GET YOUR GROCERIES OR THEY MISS THE CUTOFF, IT'S YOUR RESPONSIBILITY TO GET THEM OR THEY GET THROWN AWAY.

Grocery Delivery: Peapod (4+ days advance notice)
  1. Go to stopandshop.com and set up an account for delivery to 68 River Road (Coastline Freight).
  2. Make an account with Coastline Freight. Check their website for updated info on how to do this right! They're nice folks and respond to emails with questions.
  3. Select your food, set your delivery time, and you're good to go!
Restaurants

Options vary from time to time, but in the Pines, there's:

  • Pines Pizza
  • The Bistro
  • Canteen (the bowls are FIRE!)
  • Deli at Pines Pantry (the breakfast sandwiches here are EVERYTHING. My lunch order is the Pines Bombshell Hero, double meat, no cheese!)
  • Blue Whale (recently updated and very delicious)

Don't forget about the Grove! Lots of great options there too.

Pines Liquor Shop

I'm not a huge drinker, but there's no place I'd rather grab a bottle of bubbly. They even deliver for free and are right next to the Pantry. Excellent selection, knowledgeable staff, and shirts that are very iconic indeed.

The Serve

Fire Island Pines' soft serve shop is fabulous and flavorful. They change up the flavors frequently and IT'S VEGAN!

House Party Etiquette (How to get into that house party)

"Fire Island isn't about what you get from the experience, it's what you bring to share."

You're gonna find yourself surrounded by all kinds of things to do in Fire Island Pines, and chances are, you're going to want to stop by a home full of people you don't know and stand around by the pool or melt into a living room dance floor of sexy strangers. There's a lot of hush-hush about how to become a part of these fabulous experiences, and I'm here to demystify it for you and make your afternoons, evenings, and mornings after much more special. And I didn't use AI to write any of this, so if there's a typo, just lmk like a friend would.

Offer to help out! (In advance)

If you know about a party coming up, reach out to the hosts and see if there's anything you can do to help! Hosting in the Pines is a lot of work, and offering to get packages, bring supplies, work door, or clean up afterwards goes a long way. Pro tip: there are lots of folks you can pay to help clean up after a party as well! Booking a service like this might just be the nicest thing you can do. I work with Maria for these moments. Send her a text at +1 (631) 578-4897 and tell her Joey sent ya.

Don't show up empty-handed!

Bring a bottle of bubbly, liquor, or a bunch of mixers. These parties ALWAYS run out, and showing up with a bottle in hand is a great way to show you want to contribute right off the bat.

Be (exceptionally) nice to the door person.

I guarantee that whoever is working the door at a party you want to go to is either a begrudging volunteer or paid, so be very kind to them! It's a small community and if you're an asshole, we will find out, and we will not approve of this behavior.

If the door person says no, it's OK to ask why, but always show respect.

Chances are this door person really does want to let you in, so ask if you can help out, come back later, or if there's anything else you can do. Most of these lovely humans at the door are just here to have a good time, and want to let you in when the house isn't as full or they're allowed to.

Once inside, go say hi to someone who lives there, introduce yourself, and say thank you.

It's just good manners...like anywhere!

Calm down, DJ. Not your party? Not your music. Not your vibe? Go elsewhere.

The humans throwing the party chose their music and vibe for a reason, and while it's OK to ask what the party's about or for, it's not your place to judge it OR EVER GO UP TO THE DJ AT SOMEONE ELSE'S PARTY AND TRY TO KICK THEM OFF.* There's always something else going on in Fire Island Pines, so do what your heart asks of you.

*This literally happened to me on my birthday with one of the rudest queens I've ever met in the Pines. Fortunately, we don't see her around anymore.

Don't be a fucking mess.

Substances. Gurl. Let's talk.

  1. Substances are not necessary at all for party (or any?) enjoyment on Fire Island. If you absolutely NEED drugs or booze to have fun at a party, it must not be a very good party, and you should go find a better one.
  2. The Other Humans of Fire Island did not take PTO to be your caretaker. Take care of your damn self. Overindulged? Go home and rest. Not feeling the vibe? Don't "fix" it with a powder or a pill. Go do something more aligned with your truth. Solo beach walks are some of my absolute favorite memories, and I've never regretted getting a full night's sleep!
  3. The Other Humans of Fire Island did not take PTO to be your personal substance somm. Don't ask strangers for substances. You might not get what you want, you might get more than you asked for, and you might kill their vibe. Take care of your own needs, and if you're worried about not having substances for a party, see point 1.
  4. Choosing a substance-free journey? That's totally 100% OK, and it's OK to let other folks know too. Plenty of sober and sober-curious people have a blast in even the most raunchy of after-hours. Telling other folks you're sober means you're choosing to show up for an experience just as you are, and that's always welcome.

Help clean up during the party, and encourage others to do so.

Put a dish in the sink, clean out some cups, put things in the trash, whatever! It makes a better vibe for everyone, and the hosts notice and appreciate the gesture.

Have fun! Parties are for fun.

Bring the VIBE, babes. THE VIBE! Showing up to a party, frowning by the pool, and expecting others to make your day is dreadful behavior. Yes, even if you're hot. Especially if you're hot.

Lots of folks in the Pines can be insecure at these occasions, and if you're one of them, join the club and meet some of your fellow members! Greeting others with a hug and a hello is a wonderful way to improve your mood and make the party more fun for everyone. So if you see someone kind of down, go say hi and invite them into the fun! You never know what someone else's life is like off-island, and you can really make their world brighter by showing a little kindness.

When it's time to go, it's time to go.

It's. Not. Your. House. You sleep elsewhere. So unless you bought into their share, it's time for you to go too. Be sure to thank the hosts before you do!
